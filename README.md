# My Favorite Series and Movies

Welcome to my repository! Below is a quick overview of some of my favorite series and movies, organized using various Markdown elements.

## 📺 Favorite Series

* **Eighty-Six (86)**
  * *Genre:* Sci-Fi, Mecha, Military Drama
  * *Overview:* Features an incredible narrative exploring themes of discrimination, war, and camaraderie, backed by a phenomenal musical score.

* **Black Clover**
  * *Genre:* Action, Fantasy, Shonen
  * *Overview:* Asta’s "never give up" mindset is inspiring, and the team-based magic battles throughout the series are exceptionally hype.

* **Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood**
  * *Genre:* Dark Fantasy, Adventure
  * *Overview:* A masterclass in storytelling with a deeply thought-out alchemy system, compelling character arcs, and unforgettable plot twists.

> *"A lesson without pain is meaningless. For you cannot gain anything without sacrificing something else in return."* — **Fullmetal Alchemist**
